TatorMail privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

TatorMail is a personal app. It runs on its owner's own Windows PC and is used only with the owner's own email accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, TatorMail reads messages, labels and drafts in his Gmail accounts (through the Gmail API) and his Outlook account (through Microsoft Graph).

What it does with it

  • Sorts and summarizes new mail, and shows the summary on a page on the owner's PC.
  • Writes reply drafts into his Drafts folder, and sends a reply only after he approves it.
  • Labels, archives or moves mail to Trash. It never deletes mail permanently.
  • Sends the owner a daily summary email and short phone alerts about urgent mail.

Where data goes

To sort, summarize and draft replies, the text of a message is sent to Anthropic's Claude under the owner's own Claude accounts. Short phone alerts go through ntfy.sh. TatorMail sends mail data nowhere else. It does not sell or share it, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

What it stores

TatorMail keeps a small database on the owner's PC: message ids, sorting results, summaries and a log of its actions. It does not store message bodies, the text of drafts or attachments. Message rows and summaries are deleted after 60 days, and the action log after 90 days. Account logins are kept only in the Windows Credential Manager on that PC.

Google user data

TatorMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google, or account.live.com/consent/Manage for Microsoft.
